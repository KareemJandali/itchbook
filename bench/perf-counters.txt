40,764 page faults eliminated / 4 book constructions = 10,191 pages per book
10,191 pages x 4096 = 41.7 MB; the removed slab is 1<<20 x 40 = 41.9 MB.
Agreement 99.5% — the eliminated faults are that slab and nothing else.

instructions -1.7%: no steady-state work was removed, which is the point.
